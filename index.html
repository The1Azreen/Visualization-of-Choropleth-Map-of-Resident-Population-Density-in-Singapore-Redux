About the numbers

  • What is counted: Singapore residents (citizens and permanent residents) living in each planning area as at June of each year. Non-residents such as work-pass holders are not included, so the totals are lower than Singapore's overall population.
  • Rounding: SingStat rounds every figure to the nearest 10 before publishing. Each total shown here is a sum of those rounded figures, so it can differ slightly from SingStat's headline numbers. For example, the 2026 sum is 4,244,570, against the published 4.23 million.
  • Colour groups: areas are split into five groups, each covering about the same number of area-years. The cut-offs are set once from every year for the chosen age group, so a colour means the same number of residents in every year as you play through them. Grey areas have no residents, such as industrial estates, water catchments and islands.
  • Counts, not density: the shading shows how many residents live in an area, not people per square kilometre.
  • Boundaries: planning-area lines are from URA Master Plan 2019. SingStat reported earlier years on older master plans (1998, 2008 and 2014) and 2026 on Master Plan 2025, so small boundary differences apply. In 2000, Boon Lay and Pioneer were reported as one area, so both show the combined figure.

Population data: Singapore Department of Statistics. Boundaries: URA Master Plan 2019.
